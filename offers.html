Before you choose

Frequently asked

What's the difference between Premium and Apex?

Premium is one shared Saturday class, R680 a block. Apex is unlimited 1-on-1 access any hour, built entirely around your own gaps, with a full-refund guarantee attached — R30,000 for the semester.

What does the guarantee actually mean?

Attend every class, complete every homework set, pay on time — and if you still miss the goal you agreed on individually, tutoring continues free (or, for Apex, a full refund). The guarantee is there to protect effort and keep the tutoring accountable.

How many students are in a Premium class?

10 per class, so every student gets live 1-on-1 correction instead of getting lost in a lecture-style room.

How do I apply for Apex?

WhatsApp us — Apex is by application since it's built around your specific gaps from week one.
