About MELL

Good teaching.
A personal commitment.

Wandile Nhlumayo

Engineering Maths tutor & playbook author

Wandile is an Aeronautical Engineering student at Wits. Since February 2026, he has taught first-year engineering mathematics in person and written the Algebra and Calculus playbooks used in his sessions.

He also runs a free WhatsApp community of 400+ first-years, sharing study guides, explanatory videos and past papers.

His earlier online teaching covered Mathematics, Maths Literacy, Accounting and Physics, including IEB students and an NSC Mathematics student who passed after two unsuccessful attempts. It taught him to be patient and explain an idea in more than one way.

How we teach

Understanding first.
Confidence follows.

A concept becomes useful when you can recognise it in a new question and use it on your own.

Wandile NhlumayoEngineering Maths tutor
Author of the MELL playbooks
  1. 01

    Understand the idea.

    Learn a concept as a method you can use again, with a clear explanation of why it works.

  2. 02

    Try it for yourself.

    Apply it to a past-paper question. Your attempt shows what has clicked and what still needs attention.

  3. 03

    Correct. Practise. Repeat.

    Close the gap, then try a different question until the method holds under exam pressure.

The name

Named for a belief.

MELL is named after Meluleki, a mentor who believed high-quality education should be within reach of any student willing to put in the work.

The mark

Two forces. One grip.

Two interlocking forms represent tutor and student: independent people connected by the work and a shared goal.

Conquer the edge.

The peregrine is part of MELL’s identity: focused, agile and willing to meet the edge. We bring that intent to the work, one concept and one question at a time.
