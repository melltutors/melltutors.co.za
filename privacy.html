Privacy notice.

This notice explains how MELL Tutors collects, uses and protects personal information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Last updated 4 October 2026.

Who we are

MELL Tutors (Pty) Ltd, Reg. 2026/714649/07, is the responsible party for the personal information described here. We tutor Wits first-year engineering students in Engineering Mathematics 1 and publish study guides. Building OB, John Moffat, Yale Rd, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2017. Email: melltutors.w@gmail.com.

Playbook requests

For free downloads, we collect your email address, phone number and course. For hardcopy enquiries, we collect your email address and chosen product before directing you to WhatsApp. We record the request date, repeat requests and your optional email-update choice.

We use these details to provide and support your request. Optional marketing emails require a separate, unticked opt-in; downloading a guide does not subscribe you. You can withdraw that choice by emailing us. Under 18? Please ask a parent or guardian to make the request for you.

What we collect

  • Students and parents: your name, phone number, the course you are taking and whatever you tell us when you message us on WhatsApp or email us.
  • Tutor applicants: your name, contact details, CV and academic transcript.

We do not ask for ID numbers, bank card details or other sensitive information through this website.

Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and confirm bookings.
  • To run sessions and send the study guides you request.
  • To assess and contact tutor applicants.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who else handles it

We talk to you through WhatsApp (Meta) and Gmail (Google), so those companies process your messages under their own terms. This website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads. Cloudflare operates the download service and stores the request records for MELL. Cloudflare also stores private delivery copies of the four free playbook PDFs. Google Drive retains the original files. These providers may process data outside South Africa. MELL limits access to request records and uses protected, expiring download links; those links expire after two hours. Temporary abuse-prevention records use a keyed hash of the IP address rather than storing the raw address, and are removed after their short validity period. We do not sell request records.

Tutor applicants

Your CV and transcript are used only to assess your application for a MELL tutor role. We do not pass them on to other organisations. If you are not appointed, you can ask us to delete your documents at any time by emailing us.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or for as long as the law requires.

Under 18?

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you, or to be copied in on your messages.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email melltutors.w@gmail.com and we will respond. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Private review versions use an essential access cookie that lasts up to one day.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
