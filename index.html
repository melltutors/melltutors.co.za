Why the falcon

The peregrine conquers the edge in the air.

It's the fastest animal alive not through brute strength, but through a dive — folding in, aiming precisely, and committing to the edge instead of avoiding it.

Wits tells its students, "Wits gives you the edge." Most students experience that edge as the thing they're afraid of — the difficulty, the pace, the fear of falling behind. MELL's position is simple: rather than pretending the edge is not there, we teach the student how to move through it.

MELL is named after Meluleki — a mentor whose belief was that high-quality education should be within reach of any student willing to put in the work, not just the ones who can already afford a perfect school system.

How a session runs

The method

  • LearnThe concept is taught as a reusable system, not a one-off walkthrough.
  • TryApplied immediately to a real past-paper question, under time.
  • FailMistakes surface fast, in the room, where they're cheap to fix.
  • RelearnThe gap gets named precisely and closed — no vague "review this again."
  • Try againRepeat on a new question until it holds under exam pressure.
