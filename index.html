How we teach

Understanding first.
Confidence follows.

A concept becomes useful when you can recognise it in a new question and use it on your own.

Wandile NhlumayoEngineering Maths tutor
Author of the MELL playbooks
  1. 01

    Understand the idea.

    Learn a concept as a method you can use again, with a clear explanation of why it works.

  2. 02

    Try it for yourself.

    Apply it to a past-paper question. Your attempt shows what has clicked and what still needs attention.

  3. 03

    Correct. Practise. Repeat.

    Close the gap, then try a different question until the method holds under exam pressure.
