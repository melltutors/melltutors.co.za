Frequently asked

What's the difference between MATH1048 and MATH1049?

MATH1048A is Semester 1 (Engineering Mathematics 1A); MATH1049A is its Semester 2 continuation (Engineering Mathematics 1B). Together they make up Engineering Mathematics 1.

Does this follow the Wits lecture syllabus?

Yes — study guides and tutoring are built directly from past Wits tests and the current course structure, chapter by chapter.

Can I get help with just one topic?

Yes. WhatsApp us the specific chapter or question and we'll point you to the right study guide or session.
