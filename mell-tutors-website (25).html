Why the falcon

The peregrine conquers the edge in the air.

It's the fastest animal alive not through brute strength, but through a dive — folding in, aiming precisely, and committing to the edge instead of avoiding it. That's the model for how MELL teaches: not more hours, a sharper line of attack.

Wits tells its students, "Wits gives you the edge." Most students experience that edge as the thing they're afraid of — the difficulty, the pace, the fear of falling behind. MELL's position is that you go toward it, on purpose, with someone who's already dived.

MELL is named after Meluleki — a mentor whose belief was that high-quality education should be within reach of any student willing to put in the work, not just the ones who can already afford it.

How a session runs

The method

  • LearnThe concept is taught as a reusable system, not a one-off walkthrough.
  • TryApplied immediately to a real past-paper question, under time.
  • FailMistakes surface fast, in the room, where they're cheap to fix.
  • RelearnThe gap gets named precisely and closed — no vague "review this again."
  • Try againRepeat on a new question until it holds under exam pressure.

Before you message

Frequently asked

Who is this for?

Wits first-year engineering students currently taking MATH1049A (Engineering Mathematics IB), who want to move past a passing grade toward real command of the material.

Where and when do sessions run?

Braamfontein, Johannesburg — Building OB, John Moffat, Yale Rd, 2017. Saturdays, roughly 5 hours per session, on campus.

What does "distinction or free" actually mean?

Attend every class, complete every homework set, pay on time — and if you still miss the goal we agreed on individually, tutoring continues free. The condition exists so the guarantee rewards effort, not attendance alone.

How many students are in a class?

10 per Last Lock-in class, so every student gets live 1-on-1 correction instead of getting lost in a lecture-style room.

What's the difference between Last Lock-in and Apex?

Last Lock-in is one shared Saturday class, R680 a block. Apex is unlimited 1-on-1 access any hour, built entirely around your own gaps, with a full-refund guarantee attached — R30,000 for the semester.

Are the playbooks free?

Every playbook has a free version with the explanations for each chapter. The full version adds the worked examples, all the questions and the solutions: R400 each, or R600 for Algebra + Calculus. Tap a playbook in the Playbooks section and WhatsApp opens with a message asking for the version you want.
